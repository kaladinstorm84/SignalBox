# Northstar product handbook

Northstar is a fictional team task-management product used only for this demo.

## Plans

- Starter supports up to 5 team members and 3 active projects.
- Team supports up to 25 team members and unlimited active projects.
- Both plans include CSV exports of project tasks.
- Only Team includes scheduled weekly exports.

## Exporting tasks

A workspace administrator can open Settings > Data > Export tasks to request
a CSV export. Other workspace members cannot request exports.

Exports contain task titles, statuses, assignees, and due dates.
They do not contain uploaded attachments.

After an export is ready, its download link appears in Settings > Data.
