# Northstar support policies

These are fictional policies used only for the knowledge-base demo.

## Export availability

Export download links expire 7 days after the export becomes ready.
A workspace administrator can request a new export after a link expires.

## Support

Support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00-17:00 UTC, excluding
public holidays. Starter and Team customers can contact support from
Help > Contact support.

Include the workspace name, approximate time of the issue, and any visible
error message. Never include passwords, API keys, or the exported customer data.

## Refunds and service commitments

These references do not specify a refund policy or a guaranteed response time.
Do not promise a refund or a response-time guarantee.
